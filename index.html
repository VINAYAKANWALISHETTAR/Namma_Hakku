Citizens know what went wrong. They don't know what to do next.

A stopped pension, a pending ration card, a street nobody cleans. The hard part is the process, the authority and the legal wording.

Lakshmi, 68, Hubballi

Her pension stopped 3 months ago. She doesn't know who to write to, which law applies, or how to write it in English. In the end she gives up. NammaHakku is built so that doesn't happen.

One spoken turn becomes a ready letter

Speak in your language and receive a grounded, validated document for the right authority. The same pipeline handles civic complaints with a configurable SLA clock. Phase 1 covers pension disbursal, PDS and ration cards, and municipal complaints.

ನಿಮ್ಮ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ ಹೇಳಿ

Ready

    Six narrow agents, one citizen request

    NammaHakku is not one big model. Each agent has a trigger, a decision, a confidence threshold, a retry budget and an escalation path.

    It decides when to act, ask, retry and escalate

    Law comes from retrieval, not memory

    The model receives retrieved legal context instead of relying on memorised law. Meaning and exact legal terms are both matched, then ranked together.

    Nothing leaves without passing the checks

    Every claim must trace back to retrieved law. If a citation is not found, the system refuses or regenerates. Untick items to see how the score decides.

    Edge cases: insufficient grounding means refuse, ambiguous jurisdiction means show top candidates, a failed score means retry or manual review.
